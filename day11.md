# Week 11 Journal: Final Project Workshop

## Focus: Refine and polish final project with support.

## 1. What did feedback change in your project?
The feedback I received was: "I liked the idea and it is visually very nice. mixing 80s synthware and tron is a thing that I have never seen before."

This feedback was critical because it confirmed that the "Cyan Wireframe" aesthetic was my project's most unique feature. Instead of adding more complicated game mechanics, I focused on perfecting the visual unity between the Tron-style grid and the 80s rolling mountains. It pushed me to ensure that the car, the moon, and the terrain all shared the exact same glowing wireframe style to keep that "unseen" aesthetic consistent.

## 2. What final refinements will you make?
Since my code is now in its near-final version, my "refinements" are focusing on polish, usability, and aesthetics for the final showcase.

* **Code Stability:** Code Stability: I have finalized the drawMovingTerrain() function, using the modulo operator (%) to ensure the infinite road movement is perfectly smooth and "polished" for the viewer.
* **Visual Polish:** I have locked in the stable moon rotation (Y-axis only) to ensure the horizon looks professional and doesn't distract the player.
