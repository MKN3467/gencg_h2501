### Recreating the patterns

### Summary of Key Learnings:
The main learning was mastering translational symmetry in hexagonal geometry. The key technical realization was that hexagonal tiling requires a specialized coordinate system: the vertical distance between row centers is three-quarters of the hexagon height, and every second row must have a staggered horizontal offset (half the hexagon width). This technique is essential for achieving the perfect, close-packed tessellation required by the pattern.
